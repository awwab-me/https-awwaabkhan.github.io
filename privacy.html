Last updated: September 2026

NEXORA AI can connect to third-party AI providers such as Google Gemini, OpenRouter, and Groq. Information sent to these services may be processed according to their respective privacy policies and terms.

3. Google Sign-In

If you choose to use Google Sign-In, NEXORA AI may use Google's authentication services to allow you to sign in. We only request the permissions necessary for the features provided by the application.

4. API Keys

Users are responsible for any API keys they choose to configure in NEXORA AI. Do not share your private API keys with other people.

5. Local Data

Some application settings, chat history, or preferences may be stored locally on your computer to provide application features.

6. Third-Party Services

NEXORA AI may use third-party services. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee complete security.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions about NEXORA AI, please use the contact method provided on the official NEXORA AI website.
